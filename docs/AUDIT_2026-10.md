# SampleRateTap adversarial audit, 2026-10-09

Audited tree: `main` at `fc2949e` ("C ABI: fixed point at every engine's C boundary"), the range under scrutiny being the rational engine's landing and the C ABI reshaping (`20116c6..fc2949e`), with DspTap pinned at `ef6fdc4`. Date: 2026-10-09. Host battery: 290 ctest entries green on x86-64 (gcc 13, clang 18); no Arm, Hexagon or QEMU toolchain was available, so the embedded legs and the instruction-count ratchet were read, not run.

## Verdict

The maintainer asked whether quality suffered when the rational engine and the C ABI reshaping came in. The numeric quality did not: every datapath the audit could drive is bit-identical under every chunking, allocation-free, clean under ASan and UBSan, matches an independent scipy `upfirdn` leg from the first sample, and the bridge and async engines produce md5-identical output streams before and after the whole range in float, Q15 and Q31. What did slip is the discipline around the code. The audit raised 25 candidate findings (6 merged as duplicates into 19), and the three verification lenses confirmed 18 of the 19, disputed 1, refuted 0 and found 0 to be documented limits; 16 further nits were not put to verification. Of the 18 confirmed, 4 are major and 14 minor; there are no blockers. The two code defects that matter are both in the new code and both reachable only through unusual but undocumented-as-forbidden use: a heap-buffer-overflow in `tap::dsp::chain` when a chained stage has been `pull()`ed (F01), and a `uint8_t` schedule field that makes charter-admitted ratios with M >= 256 L run silently wrong (F02). The other two majors are claims the tree does not keep: a chain contract equation that is false for every interpolating chain (F03) and a book sentence that says the exported-symbol count is a test when nothing counts symbols (F04). The 14 minors are mostly documentation that did not keep pace with the third engine and the fixed-point levers (root README, async and bridge READMEs, the plans, the Doxyfile, a few contract numbers stated without provenance) plus three test-coverage holes. In short: the engines are sound, the contract prose and the gate story are behind them, and two latent bugs in the new substrate/stage code want a one-line fix each. Mutation testing backs the first half of that sentence: fourteen of seventeen deliberate defects planted in the rational headers were caught by the battery, and the three that survived are one class, the tie direction of the fixed-point rounding, which no vector in the battery exercises (F13).

## Confirmed findings

### F01 (major, rt-safety): chain scratch bound breaks after a chained stage is pulled

**Claim.** `tap::dsp::chain` sizes its inter-stage scratch from `detail::max_outputs_for = n*L/M + 2` and its comment calls that "the bound every stage's contract keeps: ... an interpolator exactly nL". rational's `basic_stage` keeps that bound only for states reached through `process()`/`flush()`. After its public `pull()` an interpolator or mixed stage can sit with `m_pending == 0` and up to L-1 outputs banked, so `outputs_for(n) = nL + (L-1)`. The chain exposes a public mutable `stage<I>()` and takes stages by value without `reset()`, and no document forbids driving a chained stage.

**Where.** `submodules/dsptap/include/tap/dsp/chain.h:150-158` (bound and comment), `:280-283` (mutable `stage<I>()`), `:184-199` (constructor, no reset); `rational/include/tap/sr/rational/stage.h:254-278` (`pull()`), `:595` (`m_pending = step.advance`).

**Failure scenario.** `rat::chain<up_8, up_2> c(1); c.stage<0>().pull(out, 1, pop); c.process(in, 64, out);` stage 0 writes 8*64+7 = 519 frames into a scratch of 514. Or: pull one frame from a standalone `basic_stage<float, ratio_8_3>`, move it into a `tap::dsp::chain`, `process(in, 64, out)`: 173 frames into 172.

**Evidence.** Three independent ASan harnesses, same signature: `ERROR: AddressSanitizer: heap-buffer-overflow ... WRITE of size 4 ... #0 basic_stage<float, ratio<8u,1u>>::emit(float*) stage.h:592 #1 basic_stage::process stage.h:238 #2 tap::dsp::chain<...>::run_chunk<0ul> chain.h:316 #3 chain::process chain.h:207 ... 0 bytes after 2056-byte region allocated by ... chain.h:196` (2056 bytes = 514 floats). Bound sweeps over all 14 ratios and both bridge directions: from process/flush-reached states, 0 violations for n <= 200; from pull-reached states, violations only on up_6 (excess 3), up_8 (excess 5) and 8/3 (excess 1). `git blame`: the comment blocks date from `a938556` (2026-10-01, the chain's landing), written before `basic_stage::pull()` existed. Every in-tree use of `stage<I>()` is read-only (`taps()`, `window_frames()`, `macs_per_superblock()`).

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, major: reproduced under ASan; `stage.h`'s contract block never states the bound `chain.h` attributes to it, and ordinary chain use is safe. Reproducer, confirmed, major: reproduced both variants from scratch; a legal, undocumented-as-forbidden sequence corrupts the heap inside a `noexcept` audio-thread call, but no shipped chain or C ABI route triggers it. Contract, confirmed, major: `chain.h:24-25` and `:152` assert a stage contract that `stage.h:58-62` does not make; the bridge converter stays inside the bound after pull, so a bridge-through chain is not exposed.

**Suggested fix.** Either make the stages const-only through the chain (or document that a chained stage must never be driven directly) and `reset()` stages in the chain constructor, or widen `max_outputs_for` to `n*L/M + L + 1`; in either case correct `chain.h:24-25` and `:150-154`, or add the bound to `basic_stage`'s contract and pin it.

### F03 (major, doc-drift): chain contract states an equation and a pull that do not exist

**Claim.** `chain.h:30-33` promises "a pull that delivers exactly frames_needed(k) frames yields exactly k outputs (outputs_for(frames_needed(k)) == k, and one frame fewer yields fewer)". Neither `tap::dsp::chain` nor rational's `basic_chain` has a `pull()`, and the equation is false for every chain whose first stage interpolates. `rational/PLAN.md:285-288` repeats the chain-pull wording. The tests pin only `>= k` and minimality.

**Where.** `submodules/dsptap/include/tap/dsp/chain.h:30-33`; `rational/PLAN.md:285-288`; tests at `submodules/dsptap/tests/test_chain.cpp:134-136` and `rational/tests/test_chain.cpp:236-238` (EXPECT_GE / EXPECT_LT).

**Failure scenario.** A caller of `up_2_up_8_up_3<float>` reads the contract, allocates k frames and calls `process(in, frames_needed(k), out)`: for k = 1 the chain writes 48 frames into a 1-frame buffer.

**Evidence.** `grep -n pull` over dsptap `chain.h`, rational `chain.h` and `tap_sr_rational_capi.h`: the only hit is the doc line itself; a requires-expression probe says `tap::dsp::chain` and `basic_chain` have no `pull()`, `basic_stage` does. Harnesses: "up_2_up_2 k=1 frames_needed=1 outputs_for(fn)=4 process wrote 4", "up_2_up_8_up_3 k=1 frames_needed=1 outputs_for(fn)=48 process wrote 48", "!= k in 3150 of 3200 (k,pos) cases, max excess 47"; decimator chains "!= k in 0 of 3200". The stage-level sentence (`stage.h:92-94`) is true because `pull(out, k, pop)` stops at k and `test_stage.cpp:339` pins it. `git log -L31,33` resolves to `a938556`, the decimate.h era.

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, minor: a false equation inside the contract block, but the sizing rule two lines above (`outputs_for(n)` is "exactly what the next process(n) call returns") is correct and tested. Reproducer, confirmed, major: a documented contract point stated as a number that is false in most states, carried into PLAN.md, pinned by no test. Contract, confirmed, major: the sentence was lifted from the stage contract where it holds; by the project's own rubric a contract promise that is false and pinned by nothing is major.

**Suggested fix.** Reword `chain.h:30-33` and `rational/PLAN.md:285-288` to "outputs_for(frames_needed(k)) >= k and outputs_for(frames_needed(k) - 1) < k; size out by outputs_for()"; drop "pull" from the chain contract or add a chain `pull()`.

### F02 (major, correctness): `uint8_t` advance truncates the schedule for M >= 256 L

**Claim.** `make_schedule()` stores each output's input advance as `std::uint8_t` (`stage.h:110`, cast at `:124`). `ratio<L, M>` admits any coprime 2^a 3^b pair and `stage.h:6` promises "one ratio L/M of the charter", but for `ratio<3,1024>`, `ratio<2,729>`, `ratio<9,4096>` and the like the advances are truncated mod 256: the stage dots the wrong input positions, `m_pending` wraps, R10's chunking invariance breaks and the stage goes dead, with no static_assert, throw or warning.

**Where.** `rational/include/tap/sr/rational/stage.h:110`, `:124`, `:595`; charter asserts at `rational/include/tap/sr/rational/ratio.h:77-82`; C ABI guard at `rational/capi/tap_sr_rational_capi.cpp:317`.

**Failure scenario.** `basic_stage<float, ratio<3,1024>> st(1); st.process(x, 4096, y)` emits 12 frames whose first schedule entry advances 85 instead of 341; one-frame chunking of the same stream makes 48; afterwards `frames_needed(1) = 4294964225` and the stage never produces again.

**Evidence.** Three independent harnesses agree: "ratio<3,1024>: advances 85 85 86 (sum 256, M = 1024) ... one-frame chunking produced=48, bit-identical=NO ... frames_needed(1)=4294964225"; "ratio<2,729>: advances 108 109 (sum 217, M = 729)"; control "ratio<3,512>: advances 170 171 171 (sum 512) bit-identical=yes". Reproducer's upfirdn reference built from the stage's own coefficient rows: worst deviation 8.2e-3 for 3/1024 versus 1e-17 for 3/512. `clang++ -Wall -Wextra -Wconversion -Wsign-conversion -fsyntax-only` emits 0 warnings (the `static_cast` silences it). `ratio.h` carries only the three charter static_asserts; `rational/CLAUDE.md` says "Nothing else compiles".

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, major: tried to refute via a documented restriction (none), a compiler flag (silenced) and the C ABI (protected by L, M <= 255); one slip in the finder's text, `outputs_for(4096) = 0` is the post-block state, pre-call it returns 12. Reproducer, confirmed, major: silent wrong audio plus a dead stage, bounded to M/L >= 256 which no rate pair produces. Contract, confirmed, major: no UB (`process()` sizes by `outputs_for()` before producing, the uint32 wrap is defined); the "buffer sizing overflows" part of the scenario is overstated, the rest stands.

**Suggested fix.** Widen `schedule_entry::advance` to `std::uint16_t`/`std::uint32_t` (one entry per output, no hot-path cost) or `static_assert(R::k_down / R::k_up < 256, ...)` in `make_schedule<R>()`, and say in `stage.h` which part of the charter's set a stage serves.

### F04 (major, doc-drift): the exported-symbol count is called a test; nothing counts symbols

**Claim.** `book/src/part7/proof.md:104` says "Twenty-three exported symbols, the library built with hidden visibility so that no C++ leaks into the table, and the symbol count is a test." No test, ctest entry or CI step inspects the dynamic symbol table of any of the three C ABIs; the counts live only in comments (`rational/capi/CMakeLists.txt:13`, `bridge/capi/CMakeLists.txt:13`, `async/capi/CMakeLists.txt:2`) and plan prose. `rational/CLAUDE.md:41` ("exactly 23 exported symbols; `CApi.*` pins it bit for bit") reads the same way.

**Where.** `book/src/part7/proof.md:102-104` and `:166-169`; `rational/CLAUDE.md:41`; `rational/PLAN.md:827` (M6 row, honestly "`nm -D` symbol set recorded").

**Failure scenario.** A dropped `CXX_VISIBILITY_PRESET hidden` or a non-static C++ helper in a shim leaks mangled symbols into the ABI's table and every job stays green.

**Evidence.** `ctest -N | grep -i capi` lists eleven behavioural `CApi.*` tests (3 async, 3 bridge, 5 rational), none touching symbols; `grep -rn -E 'nm -D|objdump -T|readelf|dlsym|symbol count'` over `*.yml, *.sh, *.py, *.cmake, CMakeLists.txt, *.cpp, *.h` finds only the three comment lines and a dllexport comment in `ci.yml:66`; `.github/workflows` has no `migration-gates.yml` (G10 was a one-off). The counts are right today: `nm -D --defined-only` gives 14/17/23, all `T tap_sr_*`. The reproducer rebuilt rational's shim without the visibility preset: "tap_sr_rational_ count = 23, total defined = 865" with 842 leaked `tap::sr::rational`/`std` templates, and nothing would notice.

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, minor: a stated gate that does not exist, but counts are correct and no CI job claims to run the check. Reproducer, confirmed, major: reproduced the leak scenario; the rubric's "gate that is claimed but does not run". Contract, confirmed, major: the book is the family's published proof chapter and makes an unambiguous claim; the book's own "Verify it yourself" block tells the reader to run `nm` by hand, contradicting "is a test".

**Suggested fix.** Add a ctest entry per engine (Linux/macOS) that runs `nm -D --defined-only` or `objdump -T` on the built library and compares the `tap_sr_<engine>_` set to 14/17/23 (the book's shell block already shows the command), or reword to "the symbol set is recorded" as `rational/PLAN.md:827` does.

### F06 (minor, doc-drift): "Q31 tracks double within 3.4e-9" is one vector's number

**Claim.** `stage.h:77-78` ("Contract, as numbers") and `rational/README.md:34` state the figure with no input named. It is the worst deviation on the 480-frame reference noise at peak 0.9; the shipping battery's own next test prints 1.60e-8 on full-scale drive, and other noise vectors reach 1e-8 on the named decimator profiles.

**Where.** `rational/include/tap/sr/rational/stage.h:77-78`; `rational/README.md:34`; provenance stated correctly at `rational/PLAN.md:826` and `:856-860`; pins at `rational/tests/test_fixed_point.cpp:426` (5e-9 per row on that vector) and `:544` (1e-7 on full-scale drive).

**Failure scenario.** A consumer budgets Q31 error from 3.4e-9, measures 1e-8 on full-scale program material and files a regression against an unchanged datapath.

**Evidence.** Shipping battery: "[ measured ] worst ... |Q31-double| 3.40e-09" and "[ measured ] full-scale noise, worst |q - clamp(double)|: ... Q31 1.60e-08". Three independent harnesses on other uniform-noise vectors: down_8 transparent 9.9e-9 / 9.5e-9 / 7.7e-9 at peak 0.9, 1.1e-8 at full scale (about 20 to 24 Q31 LSB); interpolators stay inside 2.6e-9 to 4.4e-9. The deviation grows with level and tap count, consistent with Q1.30 coefficient rounding.

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, minor: PLAN.md carries the provenance, stage.h and README dropped it; pins remain internally consistent. Reproducer, confirmed, minor: exceeded with merely a different seed at the same level and length; did not verify the finder's closed-form bound. Contract, confirmed, minor: the root CLAUDE.md rule "measured numbers stated with their provenance" is not followed at two places; the Q31 datapath is sound.

**Suggested fix.** State the number with its provenance ("on the reference noise, 480 frames at peak 0.9") in `stage.h` and the README, or state an input-independent bound as the contract and keep 3.4e-9 as the measurement.

### F09 (minor, correctness): async `create` maps any preset to balanced; siblings return NULL

**Claim.** `tap_sr_async_create(_format)` computes `cfg.filter = preset == 0 ? fast() : preset == 2 ? transparent() : balanced()` with no range check, so preset 3, 7, -5 or 1000000 returns a live balanced converter, while bridge and rational reject `profile < 0 || profile > 3` with NULL and pin it. The async header lists only 0/1/2 and promises NULL on invalid configuration; no test passes an out-of-range preset.

**Where.** `async/capi/tap_sr_async_capi.cpp:130-132`; `async/capi/tap_sr_async_capi.h:16-17`, `:56`; `bridge/capi/tap_sr_bridge_capi.cpp:118`; `rational/capi/tap_sr_rational_capi.cpp:296`, `:317`; `async/tests/test_capi.cpp:169-176`.

**Failure scenario.** An FFI caller passes 3 meaning "transparent" by analogy with bridge's 0..3 tags and gets balanced; the SNR and latency figures they expect do not apply, and nothing in the output says so.

**Evidence.** Three C harnesses against the built `.so`: "async preset 3/7/-5/1000000 -> NON-NULL" (reproducer: each with designed latency 0.00149996 s, preset 1's), "bridge profile 4 -> NULL", "rational profile 4 -> NULL". `git show fc2949e^` and `git log -L` trace the ternary to `c76cf20`, the srt era: pre-existing, made inconsistent by fc2949e's "one shape for three ABIs".

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, minor: inherited lenience, not a regression fc2949e introduced. Reproducer, confirmed, minor: a working converter instead of an error, no crash, no wrong samples for the design actually running. Contract, confirmed, minor: the fallback is documented nowhere; the C++ `config` has no preset field so the header's "invalid configuration" literally covers rate and channels only.

**Suggested fix.** Reject preset outside {0,1,2} with NULL in `tap_sr_async_create_format`, say so in the header, and add `EXPECT_EQ(tap_sr_async_create(48000.0, 2, 0, 3), nullptr)` to `CApi.NullAndInvalidAreSoft`.

### F10 (minor, doc-drift): bindings name a Windows DLL that the MSVC build cannot export from

**Claim.** Both ctypes bindings and the async notebooks' `_find_dso` list `tap_sr_<engine>_capi.dll` for win32, but every shim exports only through `#pragma GCC visibility push(default)` under `#if defined(__GNUC__)`; no `__declspec(dllexport)`, export macro, `.def` file or `WINDOWS_EXPORT_ALL_SYMBOLS`. The limit is written down only in a `ci.yml` comment, which keeps capi OFF on the MSVC leg.

**Where.** `bridge/notebooks/tap_sr_bridge_py.py:28`; `rational/notebooks/tap_sr_rational_py.py:45`; shims at `async/capi/tap_sr_async_capi.cpp:109`, `bridge/capi/tap_sr_bridge_capi.cpp:112`, `rational/capi/tap_sr_rational_capi.cpp:290`; `.github/workflows/ci.yml:64-73`.

**Failure scenario.** A Windows user follows the README's C ABI section, cmake produces a DLL with an empty export table, and the binding raises `AttributeError` on the first `getattr` with nothing in the docs explaining why.

**Evidence.** `grep -rn dllexport` hits only `submodules/dsptap/tools/capi/dsptap_capi.h` (DspTap's own ABI does it right with `DSPTAP_API`). `ci.yml:65-67`: "none of the three libraries carries __declspec(dllexport), so an MSVC build would link DLLs exporting nothing and gate nothing." The reproducer rebuilt the mechanism with `clang --target=x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` + `lld-link`: the pragma-only object yields 0 COFF exports, the `__declspec(dllexport)` one exports the function. No capi header, README, PLAN or book page qualifies the ABI by host; `async/README.md:16` names MSVC 19.30+ as supported.

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, minor: no MSVC here, rests on code reading plus the project's own statement; caveat, MinGW-GCC would honour the pragma, so "cannot export anything" is MSVC-specific. Reproducer, confirmed, minor: mechanism reproduced with the MSVC-compatible toolchain; the bindings actively claim the opposite of the known limit. Contract, confirmed, minor: a maintainer-facing CI comment is not user documentation; the CI leg being OFF means nothing is falsely green.

**Suggested fix.** Add a `TAP_SR_<ENGINE>_API` macro (`__declspec(dllexport)` under `_WIN32` while building, `visibility("default")` elsewhere, as `dsptap_capi.h` does) and turn the MSVC capi leg ON; or state in the three capi headers, READMEs and bindings that the C ABI is built for ELF/Mach-O hosts and drop the `.dll` branch.

### F05 (minor, test-gap): relaxed designs are never measured in Q15; the 147/160 row attains -67.6 dB

**Claim.** `rational/PLAN.md:906`, `rational/README.md:36` and `rational/CLAUDE.md:49` say every Q15 stage attains the 70 dB tier at every 70 dB profile. `test_fixed_point.cpp` pins the attained Q15 stopband only for the 1/1 designs; the relaxed designs that chains and `tap_sr_rational_create_stage_format(..., divisor, Q15, ...)` run are unmeasured in Q15, and the tightened 147/160 row (48 -> 88.2's up-2 after bridge) attains -67.61 dB in Q15 against its -71.32 dB design.

**Where.** `rational/PLAN.md:905-907`; `rational/README.md:35-36`; `rational/CLAUDE.md:49`; `rational/tests/test_fixed_point.cpp:221-224` (1/1 rows only), `:491` (0.5 dB pin); `rational/include/tap/sr/rational/design.h:33-36`, `:76`; matrix rows using `d(147,160)` at `rational/tests/coverage/matrix_rows.h` rows 91, 92, 105, 176, 189, 190.

**Failure scenario.** A consumer building the 48 -> 88.2 chain in Q15 through bridge plus the C ABI's stage constructor gets images at -67.6 dB, 2.4 dB above the tier the documentation promises for every Q15 stage; nothing in the battery would catch a further regression of a relaxed Q15 table.

**Evidence.** Two independent measurements using the shipping test's own reassembly and 16384-point grid: "economy 1/1 up_2: Q15 -72.32" (matches the pinned -72.3), "economy 147/160 up_2: double -71.32 Q15 -67.61 Q31 -71.32"; also down_2 at 147/160 -67.61, up_3/down_3/3/2 at 147/160 -69.30, 2/3 at divisors 12 and 16 -69.79. `grep -c 'q15|Q15|int16' rational/notebooks/matrix.ipynb` = 0.

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, minor: numbers reproduced in numpy/scipy; the 147/160 designs are shipped stages. Contract, confirmed, minor: two corrections to the finder's evidence, `test_capi.cpp:215` builds that stage in float not Q15, and "README.md:36" is `rational/README.md:36`; the within-family named chains' relaxed rows all reach -69.8 dB or better, inside the test's own 0.5 dB tolerance.

**Suggested fix.** Scope the claim to the 1/1 designs, or extend the Q15 pins to every relaxation row of `design.h`; for the 147/160 row consider the per-branch gain lever's Q15 `m` one step up so the tightened row attains the tier.

### F13 (minor, test-gap): no stage-level pin of the fixed-point rounding direction

**Claim.** The Q15/Q31 tables are FNV-pinned but the stage's single rounding point (`basic_stage::finalize_output`) is pinned by nothing in rational: `ImpulseReproducesTableEveryRatio` computes its expectation with `finalize_output` of the code under test, the output hashes are deliberately unpinned, and the cross-precision tolerances absorb one LSB. A change of tie direction in the stage's finalize survives the battery.

**Where.** `rational/tests/test_output_hash.cpp:12-19`; `rational/tests/test_stage.cpp:84-95`; `rational/tests/test_fixed_point.cpp:427-428` (the finder's 368-371 was off); `rational/include/tap/sr/rational/stage.h:183-191`, `:566-592`.

**Failure scenario.** A refactor that changes Q15/Q31 rounding on ties (+2^13-1 instead of +2^13 before the shift) moves output bits on every tie yet every rational test stays green.

**Evidence.** Both verifiers ran the mutation for real. Refuter: `acc -= 1` before finalize for fixed-point formats, rational capi and tests rebuilt, `ctest -L '^rational$'`: "100% tests passed, 0 tests failed out of 113"; the mutant is live (the Hash test's `ratio_2_3/q15/economy` fingerprint moved from 3224608acf65df3b to 1dfff9a41dbf6450; a 20000-frame noise sweep shows down_2 Q15 and Q31 samples off by one LSB). Contract: M3 (Q15 half-down), M11 (Q31 half-down), M12 (Q15 divided path) each SURVIVED 107/107; and crucially, the multitone of `test_output_hash.cpp` and the 480-frame reference noise produce bit-identical Q15 output under M3, so pinning those hashes would not catch these mutants either.

**Votes.** Refuter, confirmed, minor: one correction, `finalize_output` is the rounding point only for decimators and the Q15 gain paths; an L-phase stage at unity gain rounds inside `tap::dsp::dot_row`, pinned by DspTap's `SampleTraits.FinalizeRoundsHalfUp`, which this tree does not build (`TAP_DSP_BUILD_TESTS=OFF`). ## Method

**Nine dimensions.** The audit was split by concern, each run by an independent finder with its own scratchpad harnesses: rational-datapath (bit-identity, accounting, pull/flush/reset, channels, latency, the scipy `upfirdn` leg); rational-fixed-point (Q15/Q31 UB, headroom, row sums, saturation, `finalize_divided`, stopbands); rational-design-and-generators (pins re-derived, generators re-run, charter probes, relaxation tables); capi-three-engines (headers vs shims vs bindings, symbol tables, invalid arguments, buffer contracts, format refusal); tests-adversarial-mutation (mutants against the battery, gate reading, CI exclusions); family-rules-build-ci (D7, D11 to D14, D16, R4, substrate discipline, the ratchet and baselines, style gates); docs-and-claims-drift (every README, PLAN, header number and book anchor against the tree); bridge-async-regression (A/B streams across the whole range, the C ABI reshaping, baselines); rt-safety-ub-sanitizers (ASan/UBSan batteries, allocation-freedom, `noexcept`, aliasing, initialization). No finder returned nothing.

**Triage.** The finders' 25 candidates were de-duplicated by root cause into 19 (six merges, each taking the higher severity: the rational-datapath bound report into F01, the fixed-point `uint8_t` report into F02, the rt-safety contract report into F03, the capi symbol-count nit into F04, the stale-comment nit into F11, the Doxygen report into F14) and 16 nits were set aside unverified.

**Three lenses.** Each of the 19 was handed to independent verifiers: a refuter, whose job was to break the finding (look for the documented restriction, the compiler flag, the gate or the test that already covers it); a reproducer, who rebuilt the failure from the claim without reusing the finder's harness; and a contract lens, who read the finding against the headers' contract blocks, the PLANs' settled decisions and the project's severity definitions, and asked whether the behaviour was a documented limit. Eleven findings received all three votes; eight (F05, F07, F08, F11, F12, F13, F14 to F19) received the refuter and contract lenses.

**Vote rule.** CONFIRMED when every verifier confirmed; PLAUSIBLE when confirmations outnumbered refutations; a tie is DISPUTED; DOCUMENTED_LIMIT when the contract lens found the behaviour settled by a plan or header and nobody confirmed. Final severity is the majority of the verifiers' severities (F03 and F04 went major on 2:1 votes; F01, F02 unanimous major; every minor was unanimous). Outcome: 18 CONFIRMED (4 major, 14 minor), 1 DISPUTED (F12, a 1:1 tie), 0 PLAUSIBLE, 0 REFUTED, 0 DOCUMENTED_LIMIT.

**Severity words.** As the project uses them: blocker (shipped wrong output or a gate that fails to catch a regression it claims to catch), major (a contract or documented promise violated, a test that pins nothing it claims to pin, CI not running what it claims, memory corruption reachable through public members), minor (a misleading doc number, a bounded test gap, an undocumented edge-case divergence), nit (wording, stale comments, no runtime effect).
